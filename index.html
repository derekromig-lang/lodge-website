Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Finger Lakes vacation rentals and direct booking perks.

Where are Romig Retreats properties located?

Our luxury vacation rentals are located throughout New York's Finger Lakes region, including waterfront cabins on Honeoye Lake and the Seneca Lake Wine Trail, timber-frame lodges near Bristol Mountain, private acreage in Springwater, and fairway homes at Lima Country Club.

Which properties feature private hot tubs?

Four of our signature retreats include private year-round outdoor hot tubs: The Lodge at Luckenbach Hill (Springwater), Seneca Lookout (Seneca Lake), Clairmonte Cottage (Honeoye Lake), and Salem Lodge (Bristol Mountain).

Do you offer pet-friendly vacation rentals?

Yes! Both The Lodge at Luckenbach Hill (with 6 private acres and a stocked pond) and Clairmonte Cottage (with a fenced yard steps from Honeoye Lake) warmly welcome well-behaved dogs.

Why book direct instead of using Airbnb or VRBO?

When you book directly on RomigRetreats.com, you save up to 15% by avoiding third-party traveler service fees while receiving direct local support and guaranteed best rates.
